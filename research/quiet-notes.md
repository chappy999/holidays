## Dates and seasonal issues to verify

- 3 November is Culture Day, an annual national holiday; expect possible domestic travel pressure and different opening patterns.
- 2 and 9 November 2026 are Mondays. Art museums and small cultural venues often have weekly closures, but actual 2026 calendars take precedence.
- First-half November foliage varies greatly by elevation, region and year. Do not promise Kyoto's peak colour; higher inland locations may be earlier and colder.
- Mountain and rural outings need daylight margins. Coastal cycling needs a rain/wind fallback, realistic ferry connections and equipment-return time.
- Avoid ferry-dependent accommodation or a distant rural transfer immediately before the international departure. An Osaka flight usually means checking the actual airport, often KIX, and allowing its transfer time.
- Ryokan and temple stays require exact dinner/check-in times and dietary questions. Private baths, room bathrooms and tattoo acceptance are property-specific.
