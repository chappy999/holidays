## Calendar and seasonal reality

**Tuesday 3 November is Culture Day**, a national holiday. Expect domestic demand around sights and transport; confirm museum holiday opening and any substitute closure. This is a demand consideration, not a claim that all trains or hotels will sell out.

**November 2026 is not a Setouchi Triennale year under the established three-year festival pattern**; do not sell festival-only artwork or ferries as available. The island route is based on permanent art venues. The Naoshima stay is Tuesday 10–Friday 13 November, avoiding a Monday arrival, but that does not establish any individual venue's actual opening dates. Check the published 2026 calendars, ticket release rules, maintenance closures and access for every selected work.

Autumn colour varies strongly with altitude, region and weather. Early-to-mid November can suit parts of the mountains, while Tokyo and Kyoto peak colour often comes later in November or beyond. **Do not promise peak foliage**, clear Fuji views or specific weather. The Kiso Valley can be colder than the large cities; daylight is short enough to start rural walks in the morning. Check current forecasts and trail information close to travel.

Hakone sightseeing depends on transport operations, weather and possible volcanic-area access restrictions. The itinerary gives it a full day but does not require the entire multi-mode sightseeing circuit. A two-night stay also makes an onsen-focused pause plausible rather than a hurried overnight stop.

## Railway and airport reality

- The fastest shinkansen service does not stop at every station. **Odawara needs a service that actually calls there**; do not route every Tokyo–Kyoto journey as if all trains serve Hakone's gateway.
- The Tokaido shinkansen reaches **Shin-Osaka**, not every central Osaka neighbourhood. Kyoto–Osaka hotel transfers are often simpler on ordinary JR or private railway routes chosen for the hotel locations.
- Tokyo–Nagiso can be approached via Nagoya or Shiojiri, depending on train connections and the starting hotel. It requires attention to limited-express stopping patterns or slower local connections. Tsumago is not a rail station: confirm the final bus or taxi and your accommodation's meal deadline before choosing trains.
- Kyoto–Naoshima and Naoshima–Osaka include multiple transport modes. The mainland rail gateway is **Uno**, usually accessed from Okayama with a possible local-train change at Chayamachi. Confirm the ferry terminal, arrival port and island transport for the accommodation actually booked.
- Tokyo could mean Haneda or Narita. Osaka may mean Kansai International or an itinerary involving another airport/domestic connection. **Do not assume one airport from the city name**; calculate airport departure time from the booked flight, terminals, airline requirements and hotel base.
- Avoid assuming that a nationwide Japan Rail Pass will save money. Price the actual selected journeys, eligible services and any supplements against point-to-point tickets. The draft makes no pass recommendation or price claim.
- Check current seat, oversized-luggage and booking rules before ticket purchase. Confirm luggage dimensions and rail operator rules rather than using one blanket rule for all Japanese trains.

## Walking, onsen and luggage

The Magome–Tsumago walk is approximately 8 km, with uneven ground and climbs even in the commonly chosen Magome-to-Tsumago direction. Allow roughly 3–4 hours with pauses, plus access and meal time. It is **not** an accessibility-neutral option. Confirm walking appetite, footwear, medical/mobility constraints the travellers choose to share, recent trail conditions and local wildlife guidance. A poor-weather version should use the post towns and local transport rather than insist on completing the path.

Luggage forwarding could simplify either the rural or island route, using a small bag between major-city stays. Do not promise next-day arrival, collection service or luggage storage at every inn. Ask the sending and receiving accommodations about their current arrangements and delivery lead times before relying on it. Seasonal local baggage services must be checked individually.

For ryokan and onsen, establish comfort with shared versus private bathing, sleeping arrangements, tattoos if relevant, dietary requirements and fixed dinner times. A private bath option may be valuable but should not be assumed available. Traditional inns and island restaurants can require advance meal planning.
